SECTION 2   ·   SUBSECTION 2.4
APERTURE GENERATION SUBSYSTEM
2.4.1

INTRODUCTION.

The handheld aperture projector (HAP) uses AGS to link planar apertures A and B on qualified surfaces up to 300 meters (984 feet) away. Matter entering either exits the other without crossing the intervening space (figure 2.4-1).

2.4.2

FUNCTIONAL DESCRIPTION.

AGS MAIN A and B power the phase-lock oscillator (PLO); AGS RDY goes gray. With APERTURE SEL at A or B, the trigger fires the field coil driver (FCD) through L1–L4. The prongs collimate a beam to form the selected aperture on a qualified surface (figure 2.4-2). A new aperture of the same designation collapses the old.

2.4.2.1

Momentum Conservation.

An object traversing the aperture pair leaves along the exit surface normal with its entry speed unchanged (|vexit| = |ventry|).
